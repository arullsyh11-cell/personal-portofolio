## Rule Verification

### Hard Gate
- **R-02 (Copywriting):** Verified zero em dashes (`—`) in `logo-exploration.html`.
- **R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness):** Responsive 2-column grid collapses cleanly to 1 column at `<= 820px`; all interactive buttons meet `min-height: 44px`.
- **R-17 / R-18 / R-36 / R-38 (Real Content & No Fabricated Claims):** Zero fake metrics, zero testimonials, zero buzzword claims.
- **R-24 / R-26 (Interactive Elements):** All 10 action buttons (`Copy SVG` and `Download` across 5 concepts) are wired to real clipboard copy and `.svg` file download handlers.
- **R-25 (Color Contrast):** `#F8FAFC` on `#080808` and `#0c0c0e`, and `#94A3B8` on `#0c0c0e` exceed WCAG AA contrast minimums.
- **R-32 / R-39 (Accessibility & Semantics):** Semantic `<header>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, `<footer>`, single `<h1>`, `role="img"` + `aria-label` on primary SVGs, `aria-hidden="true"` on scale preview SVGs, `cursor: pointer`, and `:focus-visible` outlines.
- **R-40 (Performance):** Zero render-blocking third-party scripts; inline SVGs require no external image requests.

### Purpose-Gate & Quality Locks
- **R-01 / R-29 (Color):** Pure `#080808` canvas with `#FFFFFF` primary and `#B8C0CC` / `#94A3B8` metallic silver accents, matching `DESIGN.md` and the brand brief.
- **R-06 (Typography):** `Inter` for headings/UI and `JetBrains Mono` for technical indices (`01 / MONOGRAM`) and pixel scale labels (`16px`, `24px`, `40px`), consistent with `DESIGN.md`.
- **R-07 (Background):** Solid `#080808` without grid/dot slop, keeping focus on vector silhouettes.
- **R-31 (Intentionality):** Each concept includes a concise rationale explaining geometry, negative space, and small-size legibility.
